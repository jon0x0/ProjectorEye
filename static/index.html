Detection (all cameras)

Calibrate the eyeline

  1. Have someone stand in the detection area. A box shows on every camera that sees them.
  2. Drag the gaze pad until the projected eye looks straight at them (judge from where they stand).
  3. Tap Capture point. It records every camera that sees them, which also ties the cameras together. Repeat at 4–6 spots per camera: left, centre, right, near and far, and a few where both cameras see the person.
  4. Switch to Track test and walk the area to check.

Random mode

In Random mode (Control tab) a different eye is picked while the screen is dark, so each wake-up can be a new creature. Choose which eyes take part.
